      How to play

      Six places a day. Find each one on the satellite globe and tap your guess. There are no labels and no borders, only the planet.

      • Drag to spin the globe, pinch or scroll to zoom. Zoom stops at about the width of Florida.
      • Tap to drop a pin, then Confirm. Tap somewhere else first if you change your mind.
      • Each round scores 0 to 100 based on how close you are.
      • Rounds get harder as you go, from big famous cities to places almost nobody can find, so later rounds count more toward your day total: ×1, ×1.25, ×1.5, ×1.75, ×2, then ×2.5 for the final boss round. Perfect day: 1000.
      • One optional hint per round: hemisphere, continent, or a distance check (tap a test spot, learn how many km it is from the city). A hint halves that round's score.
      • New puzzle every midnight Eastern time. Everyone gets the same six.

      More ways to play (menu, top right):

      • Practice: the daily format with fresh places, as often as you like. Doesn't count toward stats.
      • Country Streak: tap inside each country shown. One miss ends it. For territories like Puerto Rico, tapping the country that owns it counts too.
      • Hot & Cold: each tap tells you how far you are from the mystery country's nearest border, from 🥶 freezing to 🔥 burning, and an arrow points the way. Tap inside it to win.
      • Practice modes show country borders to help you learn. The daily never does.

      Share colors (round score): 🎯 95+  🟩 80+  🟨 60+  🟧 40+  🟥 15+  ⬛ under 15. 💡 marks a hint round.

      Play it like an app: on iPhone tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
