      How to play

      • Find each place on the satellite globe. Drag to spin, pinch to zoom, tap to drop a pin, then Confirm.
      • Each round scores 0 to 100 by distance. Rounds get harder and count more toward your day (×1 up to ×2.5, max 1000).
      • One optional hint per round halves that round's score.
      • New puzzle at midnight Eastern. Everyone gets the same six.
      More: modes, emojis, tips

      Menu ☰: Archive, Practice (unlimited, no stats), Country Streak (one miss ends it) and Hot & Cold (find the mystery country by distance and direction). Practice modes show country borders.

      Share emojis: 🌟 100 · 🎯 95+ · 🔥 90+ · 🟩 80+ · 🟨 60+ · 🟧 40+ · 🟥 15+ · ⬛ under 15. 💡 marks a hint round.
